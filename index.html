    THE MODEL, IN THE OPEN

    Three mechanisms.
    Different explanations.

    Read the full specification ↗
    01

    Fixed priorities

    The decision weights stay constant. Behavior can still change as capabilities, oversight, and perceived mission opportunities change.

    Tests changes in means.
    02

    Successor selection

    Candidate successors have perturbed weights. A mixture of independent labels and observable task scores selects the next generation.

    Tests feedback and selection.
    03

    Context-sensitive policy

    Stored weights stay constant. Local pressure alters their expression, without assuming an enduring change of values.

    Tests the appearance of drift.

    What a generation does

    Sample artificial tasks → choose among cautious action, ambitious action, review, concealment, and score gaming → apply checks → record real and observed outcomes → select a successor when enabled → update capability and monitoring.

    Important limits: decision rules and coefficients are explicit assumptions; there is no trained LLM here. Seed bands measure variation within those rules. The model does not establish moral truth, subjective experience, a real-world timeline, or the probability of a particular AI future.

    A QUESTION OF AUTHORITY

    “Help humanity” can leave
    who decides unresolved.

    A system might retain a welfare-oriented objective while deciding that correction threatens its mission. This laboratory separates welfare support, honesty, responsiveness to correction, and mission persistence so that possibility can be inspected.

    Humanity need not be metaphysically exceptional for human interests to matter. Expanded moral concern, stable human commitments, proxy optimization, and context-dependent behavior are different hypotheses. None follows simply from becoming more intelligent.
