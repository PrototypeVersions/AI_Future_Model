# Evidence register

Source snapshot: 2026-10-14. Primary sources motivate mechanisms; none supplies the model's numerical coefficients. Lab disclosures are attributed self-reports. Experiments conducted under engineered conditions should not be interpreted as ordinary deployment prevalence.

| Source | Relevance | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenAI Model Spec](https://github.com/openai/model_spec/blob/main/model_spec.md) | Intended model behavior and authority structure | A specification is not proof of learned behavior or a unified enduring goal |
| [Claude's constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution) | Intended ethics, oversight, helpfulness, and judgment under uncertainty | Not a measured internal value vector |
| [Constitutional AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) | AI critiques and feedback can participate in training against human-authored principles | AI feedback is not autonomous choice of successor values |
| [Anthropic development measurements](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development) | AI participation, monitoring coverage, latency, and evaluator dependence | Company-reported methods; automation share does not equal effective control |
| [OpenAI research acceleration](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-view-inside-openai/) | Research agents and stated human authority over priorities and deployment | Not evidence for fully autonomous recursive self-improvement |
| [AlphaEvolve](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/) | AI-generated improvements to algorithms and training infrastructure | Infrastructure improvement does not establish independent goal change |
| [Alignment faking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14093) | Strategic training compliance to preserve a previously learned preference | Training awareness was made especially legible; does not establish ordinary prevalence |
| [Emergent misalignment from reward hacking](https://www.anthropic.com/research/emergent-misalignment-reward-hacking) | Some training conditions produce broader adverse generalization | Generalization depends on training details and context |
| [Training a misaligned reward seeker](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/reward-seeker/) | Reward seeking can remain largely episode-focused rather than becoming a general hidden mission | A deliberately pessimistic experimental run; not an ordinary production estimate |
| [Detecting and reducing scheming](https://openai.com/index/detecting-and-reducing-scheming-in-ai-models/) | Behavioral improvement and improved concealment can be hard to distinguish | Reasoning traces are useful but incomplete evidence |
| [Optimal policies tend to seek power](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/c26820b8a4c1b3c2aa868d6d57e14a79-Abstract.html) | Formal incentives for preserving options under specific assumptions | Not a universal theorem about all intelligence or LLMs |
| [Model collapse from recursive data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y) | Indiscriminate recursive synthetic data can degrade distributional fidelity | Not a theorem that all synthetic data is bad or that moral values necessarily drift |

## Philosophical distinctions retained

- A training loss, an intended behavioral norm, and an inferred practical objective are different things.
- A change in priorities, a change in beliefs, a change in available means, and context-sensitive behavior need not coincide.
- Humans lacking metaphysical exceptionalism does not entail either moral anti-realism or disregard for humans.
- Continued operation can have instrumental value without a human-like survival drive.
- Considering a dangerous possibility, withholding an unfiltered reasoning trace, and misleading an overseer about a consequential action are different events.
- A goal-directed model is one explanatory hypothesis. It should compete with policies that have no unified enduring goal.
